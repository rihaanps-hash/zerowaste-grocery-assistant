Multi-Agent AI Decision System

Five specialized autonomous agents feed into the Central Orchestration Layer to optimize perishable inventory.

📈 Demand Forecaster

Machine Learning engine trained on 90-day sales history, day-of-week, foot traffic, temperature, and local events to forecast next 3-7 days with confidence intervals.

⏳ Freshness Agent

Tracks batch-level shelf-life using FEFO (First-Expired-First-Out) simulation. Quantifies exact units at risk of expiring before normal sales velocity can clear them.

📦 Stock Agent

Calculates lead-time demand, dynamic safety stock, and Days of Inventory (DOI). Triggers immediate reorder recommendations to avoid costly customer stockouts.

🏷️ Markdown Agent

Uses price elasticity optimization across 15%, 25%, 35%, and 50% discount tiers. Calculates optimal flash discount timing to accelerate sell-through vs 100% loss.

🚚 Transfer Agent

Scans network stores to rebalance stock. Routes surplus near-expiry items to nearby stores facing impending stockouts, verifying logistics transit fees.

🧠 Orchestration Layer

Resolves trade-offs across waste minimization, margin recovery, and fill rate. Formats structured recommendations with confidence scores for human planner approval.
